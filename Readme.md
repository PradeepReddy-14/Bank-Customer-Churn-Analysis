# 🏦 Bank Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Bank Customer Churn Analysis is a data analytics project focused on identifying customer churn patterns and understanding the factors associated with customer attrition.

The project analyzes customer demographics, product engagement, account activity, credit scores, and geographical distribution to identify customer segments with higher churn rates and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn patterns and retention rates.
- Identify customer segments with higher churn rates.
- Examine the relationship between customer demographics and churn.
- Analyze product engagement and credit card ownership.
- Explore customer activity, account balance, and tenure.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data inspection and preprocessing.
- **MySQL:** Data querying, analysis, aggregations, joins, and subqueries.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains information about **10,000 bank customers** across three geographical regions.

Key attributes include:

- Customer ID
- Age and Gender
- Geography
- Credit Score
- Account Balance
- Estimated Salary
- Tenure
- Number of Products
- Credit Card Ownership
- Active Membership
- Customer Exit Status

## 🔍 Key Analysis & Findings

- Total customers analyzed: **10,000**
- Exited customers: **2,037**
- Retained customers: **7,963**
- Overall churn rate: **20.37%**

### Important Findings

1. Female customers recorded a higher churn rate (25.07%) compared with male customers (16.46%).
2. Inactive members had a churn rate of 26.85%, compared with 14.27% among active members.
3. Customers without credit cards had a churn rate of 20.81%, while customers with credit cards had a churn rate of 20.18%.
4. Customers aged 29–47 represented an important segment in the churn analysis.
5. Germany recorded the highest average customer account balance among the three geographical regions.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Develop targeted retention campaigns for customer segments with higher churn rates.
- Improve engagement among inactive customers.
- Introduce personalized banking services based on customer needs.
- Strengthen customer relationships through periodic follow-ups.
- Monitor churn rates across demographic and geographical segments.
